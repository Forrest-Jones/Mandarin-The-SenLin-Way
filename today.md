## 3 · New (3½ min)
**Actors: d t n l**

Tongue-tip sounds. Same rule: d has no puff, t does. Meet the six simple finals — each one is a SET, a place your movies happen in.
- Actor **d-** (dà 大): like the t in “stop” — no puff. Cast: Darth Vader
- Actor **t-** (tā 他): like the t in “top” — big puff. Cast: Thor
- Actor **n-** (nǐ 你): as in English. Cast: Neo
- Actor **l-** (lái 来): as in English. Cast: Luke Skywalker
- Set **-i** (nǐ 你): “ee”; after z c s zh ch sh r it is a buzzed “zz”. Location: your workplace
- Set **-u** (bù 不): “oo” with tight round lips. Location: your grandparents’ house
- Set **-ü** (nǚ 女): say “ee” and round the lips (spelled u after j q x y). Location: a best friend’s house

**Task:** Say nǚ (woman) vs nù (angry): the ü is “ee” with rounded lips. Cast d t n l and your six basic sets.
