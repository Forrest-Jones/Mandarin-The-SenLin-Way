## 3 · New (3½ min)
**Actors: zh ch sh r — the curled sounds**

Retroflex: curl the tongue tip up and back. The “i” after these is not “ee” but a buzz: zhī chī shī rì.
- Actor **zh-** (zhōng 中): like “j” in “judge” with the tongue tip curled back. Cast: Jackie Chan
- Actor **ch-** (chī 吃): like “ch” in “church” with the tongue curled back. Cast: Charlie Chaplin
- Actor **sh-** (shì 是): like “sh” in “shirt” with the tongue curled back. Cast: Sherlock Holmes
- Actor **r-** (rén 人): like the “r” in “rug” with a hint of the “s” in “measure”. Cast: Rocky Balboa
- Set **-ong** (zhōng 中): “oong”. Location: a police station
- Set **-er** (èr 二): “are” with a curled tongue. Location: a restaurant

**Task:** Cast the four curled-tongue actors. Say 是 shì ten times: it is the most common verb you will ever use.
