## 3 · New (3½ min)
**Actors: b p m f**

Every initial sound becomes an ACTOR in your memory movies. The first four are lip sounds. Notice b/p differ only by the puff of air.
- Actor **b-** (bā 八): like the p in “spin” — no puff of air. Cast: Batman
- Actor **p-** (pá 爬): like the p in “pin” — strong puff of air. Cast: Pikachu
- Actor **m-** (mā 妈): as in English. Cast: Mario
- Actor **f-** (fēi 飞): as in English. Cast: Frodo
- Set **-a** (mā 妈): open “ah”. Location: your childhood home
- Set **-o** (wǒ 我): “aw” with rounded lips (only after b p m f). Location: your current home
- Set **-e** (hē 喝): “uh” in the throat, unrounded. Location: your school

**Task:** Hold a tissue in front of your mouth: it should move for p, not for b. Then cast your first four actors below.
