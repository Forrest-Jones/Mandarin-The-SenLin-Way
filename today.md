## 3 · New (3½ min)
**The four tones (and the fifth)**

Mandarin is a tonal language: the pitch pattern of a syllable changes the word. “mā” is mother, “mǎ” is horse. Before a single character, own the tones.
- First tone: high & flat  ˉ — sing it like a doctor’s “ahhh”. Room: outside the front door
- Second tone: rising  ˊ — like a surprised “what?”. Room: the kitchen
- Third tone: low dip  ˇ — low and creaky, like a doubtful “we-ell…”. Room: the bedroom
- Fourth tone: falling  ˋ — sharp, like a command: “No!”. Room: the bathroom
- Neutral tone: light & short — quick and unstressed, borrows pitch from the syllable before. Room: the hallway

**Task:** Hum each contour with your hand drawing the shape in the air. Then say the “ma” series five times, slowly.
