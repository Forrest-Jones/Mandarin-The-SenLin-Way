## 3 · New (3½ min)
### 木  mù  — tree
Props: 木 tree (a tree)
Scene: Mario, in the bathroom of your grandparents’ house, plants a single TREE (木) and waters it — the first tree of your forest.
### 林  lín  — woods
Props: 木 tree (a tree), 木 tree (a tree)
Scene: Luke Skywalker, in the kitchen of a farm, stands between TWO TREES (木木) — a small grove: WOODS.
### 森  sēn  — forest
Props: 木 tree (a tree), 木 tree (a tree), 木 tree (a tree)
Scene: Superman, outside the front door of a hotel, is lost among THREE TREES stacked high (木木木) — a whole FOREST. 森林 sēnlín = forest.

**New words**
- 森林 sēnlín — forest

**Deal desk (business Mandarin)**
- 董事长 dǒngshìzhǎng — chairman of the board · Address as 王董 (Wáng dǒng) after the surname.
- 总经理 zǒngjīnglǐ — general manager / CEO · 王总 (Wáng zǒng) is the universal respectful address for any boss.
- 很荣幸认识您，王总。  Hěn róngxìng rènshi nín, Wáng zǒng.  — It is an honour to meet you, Mr Wang.

## 5 · Quiz (1 min)
1. **森** — What does it mean?  (a) tree  (b) woods  (c) kneel  (d) forest
2. **木** — How is it pronounced?  (a) tuǒ  (b) mù  (c) sēn  (d) lín
3. **lín** — which one is it?  (a) 林  (b) 叼  (c) 木  (d) 森
4. **森林** — How is it pronounced?  (a) zìjué  (b) guòmǐn  (c) sēnlín  (d) rènwu

Answers: 1d 2b 3a 4c
